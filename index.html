Chapter One

Before You Knew I Was Watching

♪ Music Comes and Goes (In Waves) — Greg Laswell soft, beneath the words
🌊
Your Hebrew name Galit means waves of joy. And this song — "Comes and Goes (In Waves)" — plays underneath these first words for a reason. You, Jojo, have always come in waves. Not quiet ones. The real kind.

I first met you in your Saba's basement.

Your momma was figuring out what came next — you know, the kind of figuring out that takes everything a person has. And there you were, on the couch, teaching me everything I never knew I needed to know about Doc McStuffins. Every song. Every character. With full commitment.

You just being silly little… you. Probably no idea I'd remember your name. Definitely no idea I'd remember you forever.

But I did.

I remembered the way you existed in that space like you owned it — quietly, completely, on your own terms. Even then. Even then.

Back then, you would've known me as Samiah. But I'll get to that.

Chapter Two

Her Momma, Ilana

I want to stop here for a second, Jojo, because you need to hear this.

The woman raising you? She is one of the most remarkable people I know. What your momma Ilana has built — for you, for your sister, for herself — that's not luck. That's grit. That's love with its sleeves rolled up. That's waking up every day and deciding to try even when trying is hard.

I am truly in awe of what she has done.

And I need you to know — when life gets heavy, when it feels overwhelming, when you don't know what comes next — you look at your momma. You channel your inner Ilana. Because that woman? She figured it out. She always does.

And so will you. Because you are her daughter.

Chapter Four · a little personal

On Names, and Knowing Yours

♪ Music I Don't Know My Name — Grace VanderWaal soft · ukulele · gentle opening
✨
A note on this song Grace VanderWaal wrote "I Don't Know My Name" at 12 years old, about going through phases, changing, and finally figuring out who she actually is. She performed it on a ukulele. She won America's Got Talent. She was, in almost every way, a lot like a certain Jojo Bean.

Okay. I promised I'd get back to the name thing.

When you met me, I was Samiah. And for a long time, that's who I was — or at least, who I was calling myself. I'd changed my name years before, honestly as a kind of rebellion. A way of putting distance between myself and a past that was… hard. My mother wasn't like yours, Jojo. Not even a little. And for a while, a different name felt like armor.

But here's what I figured out eventually: my birth name is Kaska. And in Greek — or Polish, depending on the day you ask — it means pure.

Pure. My name. All along.

Once I stopped running from it, I realized the name wasn't the problem. The name was actually the truth. I was the pure one. My heart. My intentions. Not hers.

So I took it back. I reclaimed it as my own. Kaska. Kas. Pompom, to certain very important people. You know who you are.

♪ Transition → Memories — Maroon 5 bring up gently · let it settle

I'm telling you this because you are standing at the beginning of your own name story.

Georgia. Jojo. Jojo Bean. Bean. Beanie-Weanie. Meanie Head — yes, I said it. And now, Galit.

Every single one of those names is real. Every single one is you. You don't have to pick just one and stay there forever. You don't have to know right now which one fits best. You're allowed to try them all on, wear the ones that feel good, and grow into the rest.

What matters — the only thing that actually matters — is that whoever you are under any of those names, you own it. Fiercely. Freely. Completely.

Ride the waves, Galit. As wildly as you want.

Within reason. Mostly. 😉

Note: Photos of her at different ages here work beautifully — showing her evolution from tiny Doc McStuffins fan to the person she is now. A natural before-and-after without being heavy-handed about it.

Chapter Six

What I Want You to Know

♪ Music I'd Be Lying — Greg Laswell bring up, let it breathe · let the lyric land

I want to be honest with you, Jojo Bean. Because you're old enough now, and you deserve honesty more than you deserve something wrapped up neat.

I don't know exactly what my future looks like. You know that. Your momma knows that. And I'm not going to pretend otherwise, because that's not who I am and it's not what you need from me.

What I do know is this:

I am not going anywhere that I don't have to. I am here — for your momma, for your sister, and for you — for as long as I am here. Not because it's easy. Because it's where I want to be. I'd be lying if I ran another way.

"There is not much that you can do, to get me to, run away from you…"

And I need you to know that the time we have had — the golf carts, the bowling ball theft, the bug jokes, Lady Ophelia choosing you — all of it — has mattered to me more than I know how to say.

Every single moment. I was paying attention.

I still am.
